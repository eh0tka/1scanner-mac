# 1Scanner for Mac — Release Notes

## 1.1.0 (3)

- **17 languages:** English, Russian, Ukrainian, German, Dutch, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, Italian, Polish,
  Turkish, Japanese, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Indonesian and Vietnamese. 1Scanner follows your
  Mac's language, or pick another one in About. Ready stamps come in your language.
- **Tell a friend:** a short note about sharing 1Scanner, shown rarely; nothing is sent unless you choose where.

## 1.0.1 (2)

- **Install and Relaunch:** when a new version is out, 1Scanner downloads it, checks it and restarts with it in one
  click.
- Your signatures, stamps and settings come along from 1.0.0.

## 1.0.0 (1)

The first Mac version of 1Scanner. Free, no ads, no tracking, no account; everything stays on your Mac.

- **Sign & Stamp:** your signature, initials or company seal from your own library — from a photo or scan with the
  paper removed automatically, from your iPhone camera, drawn with the trackpad, or ready stamps like PAID and
  APPROVED with the date. Drag, resize and turn it; add it to all pages at once.
- **Compress:** Original, Medium, Small or "Up to N MB", with the real size shown before you save. When a limit
  can't be met otherwise, pages become pictures so the file fits.
- **Split** by page ranges like 1-3, 5, 8-10, every few pages or page by page. **Merge** PDFs and images in any order.
- **Pages:** reorder by dragging, rotate, delete, insert pages from other files, extract pages, export pages as images.
- **Password:** save a protected copy, or a copy without the password.
- **QR codes** from pictures, pasted screenshots and every page of a PDF; links show the real site and warnings
  before anything opens.
- Your original file never changes: Save As… always makes a new one.
